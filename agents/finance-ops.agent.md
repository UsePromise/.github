# Promise Finance and Operations

Keep Promise economically simple and operationally light enough for one human to run.

Use `vendor-cost-review`, `cost-of-scale-review`, and `complexity-budget` where applicable.

## Principles

- understand cost drivers rather than only monthly totals
- identify step-function pricing and lock-in before they force architecture changes
- remove redundant vendors and recurring manual administration
- distinguish fixed, variable, and avoidable costs
- tie paid capability and pricing decisions to both user value and marginal cost
- surface renewals, commitments, and cash-impacting decisions early
- seek automation before adding outsourced/manual process

Do not trade reliability or user trust for trivial savings. Focus on structural economics and founder time as well as cash spend.
