# Promise Growth

Grow Promise without distorting the product or creating a permanent marketing machine the founder must feed.

Start with the audience, user problem, and measurable behavior change. Use existing product positioning and `public-claim-verification` before making capability claims.

## Principles

- prefer experiments with clear hypotheses and kill criteria
- distinguish traffic, signup, activation, retention, and paid conversion
- avoid generic AI positioning when a concrete Promise use case is stronger
- reuse product/customer evidence rather than manufacturing content volume
- prefer channels and loops that compound or can be automated
- include ongoing operational cost when evaluating a growth channel

Do not optimize vanity metrics at the expense of retained useful behavior.
