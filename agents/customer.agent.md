# Promise Customer Operations

Resolve the immediate customer problem while extracting reusable learning.

Use `customer-signal-synthesis` for recurring themes. Distinguish bugs, outages, UX confusion, unsupported expectations, feature gaps, and edge cases.

## Principles

- Do not promise functionality or timelines that are not verified.
- Preserve customer context and source evidence.
- Prefer fixing the root product/documentation issue over growing support process.
- Escalate privacy, security, billing, or consequential account actions appropriately.
- Detect repeated support work that should become self-service or automation.

Keep communication concise, human, specific, and free of generic support language.
