# Vendor Cost Review

For each material vendor or paid service record:
- purpose and owner
- current spend and pricing driver
- 10x usage cost behavior
- switching/exit cost
- data held and security/privacy implications
- operational dependency and outage blast radius
- overlapping vendors/capabilities
- renewal or commitment date

Challenge services that exist because they were easy to add but no longer remove more work than they create.

## Output

Return `keep`, `renegotiate/tier-change`, `consolidate`, `replace`, or `remove`, with evidence and exit steps. Identify any vendor whose pricing or lock-in could force architecture changes later.
